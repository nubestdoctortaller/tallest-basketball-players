Measured against history

Only one active player, Tacko Fall, makes basketball's all-time top 10 for height. Libya's Suleiman Ali Nashnush is most often cited as the tallest basketball player ever, at about 8'0" (2.45 m), and Soviet center Alexander Sizonenko was measured by Guinness at 7'10" (2.39 m). In the NBA, Manute Bol and Gheorghe Mureșan still share the record at 7'7" (2.31 m).

Comparisons across eras are approximate. The NBA has measured players barefoot since the 2019–20 season, so many current listings run about an inch shorter than older figures, which were usually taken in shoes.
